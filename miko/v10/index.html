Straight answers

The questions stylists actually ask before they start.

Is this for me if I rent a chair or a suite?

Yes — the ladder starts at the chair, not the lease. If you're behind the chair and want to run it like a business, the score tells you which stage you're actually in.

I'm fully booked already. Why would I need this?

Fully booked is exactly when the math matters most. Booked-out stylists are the ones most likely to be underpriced — the workshop exists because being busy and being profitable are different problems.

How much time does it take?

The score takes about 3 minutes. The workshop is one live session. The group program is weekly calls plus applying playbooks to your real numbers — designed to fit around a book of clients, not replace it.

Do I need to be planning to open a salon?

No. Stages 1 and 2 are for stylists who may never want a lease — owning your numbers, your pricing, and your book is its own destination. Stages 3 and 4 are for when you do.

What does the free score actually tell me?

Where you stand across pricing, rebooking, and owner-readiness — your stage on the chair-to-CEO path, and the one thing to fix first. Free, instant, no call required.
